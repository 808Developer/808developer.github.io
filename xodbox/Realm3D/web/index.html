MARTIAL ARTS COMMAND LIST

PLAYER 1 (KAI - AZURE DRAGON)

  • W / S: Advance / Retreat toward opponent
  • A / D: 3D Sidestep Circle Left / Right
  • J (Tap 3x): 3-Hit Jab → Cross → Spinning Backfist
  • K (Tap 2x): Tornado Roundhouse → Dragon Axe Kick
  • L (Hold): WEB Forearm Guard / Parry
  • Left Shift: Evaporative Dash / Dodge
  • U or SPACE: Chi Blast / Dragon Finisher

PLAYER 2 (LOCAL PvP CONTROLS)

  • ↑ / ↓ Arrows: Advance / Retreat
  • ← / → Arrows: 3D Sidestep Circle Left / Right
  • N or Numpad 1: 3-Hit Demon Claw Combo
  • M or Numpad 2: Heavy Demon Roundhouse Kick
  • , (Comma) or Num 3: Shadow Guard Block
  • . (Period) or Num 0: Shadow Step Dodge
  • Enter or / (Slash): Crimson Nova / Finisher
💀 FINISHING MOVES ("FINISH HIM!" PHASE): When an opponent's health hits 0, they enter a dazed stagger state for 6 seconds. Step close and press [U / SPACE] for Dragon Ascension (rapid flurry + uppercut launch + meteor slam) or [K] for Plasma Nova Obliteration! Press [ESC] or [P] anytime to pause.
