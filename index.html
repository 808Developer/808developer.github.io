How to Play & Controls

  • • Select & Move: Click a piece to reveal highlighted valid destination squares, then click a target square to move.
  • • Camera Orbit: Click and drag anywhere across the 3D canvas to rotate 360° around the board.
  • • Zoom: Scroll your mouse wheel or pinch-to-zoom on touch screens.
  • • Procedural Spiders: 12 live arachnids crawl through the chamber, bouncing organically off both the perimeter walls and the chessboard edges.
  • • 100% Offline: Operates entirely without external internet connections or third-party libraries.
